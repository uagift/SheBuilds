# 👩🏽💻 SheBuilds Rwanda — Interactive Registration Experience

> **Codveda Internship — Level 1, Task 2: Interactive Form**  
> *A user-centered, responsive, and accessible registration experience for a fictional girls-in-technology community in Rwanda.*

---

## 🌍 Product Concept & Vision

**SheBuilds Rwanda** is an opportunity community dedicated to empowering young women and girls in Rwanda through technology learning, mentorship, career opportunities, and supportive sisterhood.

* **Core Motto**: *Learn. Build. Grow.*
* **Main Message**: *Your future starts with one step.*
* **Emotional Goal**: To make every aspiring young woman feel: *"I belong here."*

Instead of treating this as a routine "HTML form assignment," this project is engineered as an encouraging entry portal that minimizes friction, guides the user through data entry with helpful micro-interactions, and celebrates their registration.
